# IP, Data Rights, and M&A Proof Pack

QST: QST-356  
Status: Evidence collection pending; not legal approval

## Purpose

This pack separates implemented assets from unproven ownership, permitted-use, and market-value claims. It is a due-diligence index, not a claim that Questra or its assets are cleared for transfer or public distribution.

The canonical machine-readable register is `docs/qst/IP_DATA_RIGHTS_PROOF_PACK.yaml`. It contains no personal data, secrets, signatures, or contract copies.

## Data Rights Register

Questra does not treat user Quest, Mission, Task, Trail, Arc Memory, chat, media, or consent data as freely transferable company property. Processing, aggregation, sharing, model improvement, export, and deletion require separate lawful purpose and consent/contract analysis.

Current blockers are recorded in `docs/qst/BETA_LEGAL_SIGNOFF.yaml`: account deletion, export, correction, withdrawal, DPA/subprocessor review, contacts, retention, and human legal sign-off are incomplete.
