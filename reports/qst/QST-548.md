- Source: user report that entered text is unreadable on several forms, after the QST-537–546 review in QST-547.

## Finding and implementation

The forgot-password and reset-password fields specified white input text but inherited the global white `InputDecorationTheme.fillColor`. The login and signup fields already specified a dark fill, and the Arc chat field uses a dark composer. The recovery fields now pair white text with the existing midnight-navy auth surface and sky-blue leading icons. No authentication, database, or email behavior changed.
