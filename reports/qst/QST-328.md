# QST-328 Arc General Conversation and Consent-based Quest Routing

## Status

Implemented locally. Hosted Supabase Edge Function deployment and Android/Web
device evidence remain pending.

## Summary

Arc chat now distinguishes general questions, conversation support, new Quest
intent, and active Quest support. General and supportive conversations answer
first and cannot expose Quest mutations. A safe multi-step wish may show a
consent CTA, but no Quest is generated or saved until the existing confirmation
flow is completed by the user.

## Changes

- Added a versioned structured intent, confidence, consent CTA, related Quest,
  clarification, and safety contract to `arc-chat`.
- Minimized journey context by intent. Full Quest context is limited to active
  Quest support; ordinary conversation receives no journey records or memories.
- Removed template-based Quest fallback behavior from the Edge Function.
- Suppressed Quest suggestions, clarification questions, and change proposals
  when the classified intent does not permit them.
- Added session-level suppression when the user chooses to continue only as a
  conversation.
- Filtered related Quest and Mission identifiers through the supplied owned
  context and failed closed for blocked or malformed output.
- Added the consent boundary to the Arc Constitution.

## Residual Risks

- Session decline suppression is intentionally local to the current Arc screen;
  durable preference behavior requires a separate privacy-reviewed decision.
- Intent classification remains conservative and should be evaluated with a
  Japanese conversation corpus before Beta release.
