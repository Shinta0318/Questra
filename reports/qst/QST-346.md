# QST-346 Gentle Recovery and Non-Coercive Arc Policy

## Status

Implemented locally.

## Summary

Mission and Quest delay signals now behave as gentle route-adjustment prompts
instead of pressure notifications. Signals carry explicit recovery options and
a pressure budget so Questra can offer rest, smaller steps, and deadline review
without framing delay as failure.

## Changed Files

- `apps/mobile/lib/features/signal/mission_signal_model.dart`
- `apps/mobile/lib/features/signal/mission_signal_service.dart`
- `apps/mobile/test/mission_signal_service_test.dart`

## Acceptance Notes

- Overdue Quest signals include rest, deadline review, and five-minute-step
  options.
- Stale Mission signals include rest, five-minute-step, shrink, and deadline
  review options.
- Calm suggestions explicitly allow the user to rest.
- Signal pressure budgets cap generated interventions:
  - quiet: 1
  - balanced: 2
  - frequent: 3
- Tests reject coercive phrases such as "必ず", "今すぐ", "失敗", and
  "Arcが寂しい" in generated Signal copy.
- Signal metadata can distinguish low and medium pressure interventions.
