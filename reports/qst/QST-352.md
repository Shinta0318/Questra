# QST-352 English Productization and International Format Matrix

## Status

Implemented as an internationalization foundation. Japanese remains the only
declared Beta locale; English release readiness is intentionally blocked until
visible copy, generated Arc copy, legal content, and localized E2E evidence are
complete.

## Summary

Added a single locale-aware formatting boundary for dates, times, currency,
Mission pluralization, Monday/Sunday week-start differences, and future RTL
smoke testing. The service now distinguishes the US and UK defaults instead of
silently applying US currency behavior to all English locales.

- `apps/mobile/test/qst_352_locale_format_service_test.dart`
- `docs/product/international_format_matrix.md`
- `tools/qst/verify_international_format_matrix.dart`

## Acceptance Notes

- `ja`, `en_US`, and `en_GB` use a common formatting API.
- Japanese keeps a 24-hour clock and JPY default.
- English uses locale 12-hour time formatting; `en_GB` defaults to GBP while
  `en_US` defaults to USD.
- The matrix explicitly records that English UI and Arc copy are not yet
  product-ready.
- RTL is detected for future navigation and focus smoke testing, but is not
  presented as a supported launch locale.
