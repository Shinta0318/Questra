# QST-541 Unconfirmed Signup Login Recovery Guidance

Status: In Progress / local_partial

## Observed cause

The hosted Beta project was checked on 2026-10-06: Confirm email was enabled, custom SMTP was disabled, Site URL remained `http://localhost:3000`, and Redirect URLs was empty. Auth logs showed `Mail.send` after a successful signup request, but not inbox delivery or link completion. A later failed token request does not distinguish an unconfirmed address from bad credentials.

## Local change

The login error now gives a generic, conditional reminder to open the confirmation email and points to the existing resend path. It does not inspect or disclose account existence or confirmation status. The Supabase settings and accounts were not changed.

## Remaining external gate

Configure a production-suitable SMTP sender and exact redirect allowlist for the final HTTPS origin, then use dedicated accounts to prove receipt, confirmation, and first explicit login on a clean candidate. Until that proof exists, keep External Beta NO-GO. Do not disable Confirm email to work around mail delivery.
