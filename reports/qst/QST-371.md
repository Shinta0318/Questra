# QST-371 Physical Device Accessibility and Japanese IME Evidence

## Result

Evidence capture and verification tooling completed. Physical execution is still
pending because no Android device was available to the current environment;
External Beta remains blocked.

## Review Finding

The previous physical gate checked only whether field names such as `talkback:`
or `japanese_ime:` existed in YAML. A pending template could therefore satisfy
the shape check without proving execution. That weak gate has been removed.

## Implemented

- Added a recorder that requires a clean worktree and candidate SHA equal to
  HEAD and the Beta candidate manifest.
- Required six distinct checks: physical Android journey, TalkBack, Japanese
  IME, 200% text, compact layout, and reduced-motion/haptics behavior.
- Required privacy review and evidence files under `artifacts/qst371/`.
- Recorded byte size and SHA-256 for every screenshot/video/log artifact.
- Strengthened the accessibility release verifier to invoke the evidence hash
  gate rather than search for YAML tokens.
- Kept emulator and automated test output explicitly ineligible as physical
  evidence.

## Validation

- Static evidence contract verifier: see final QST validation result.
- Focused Flutter contract test: see final QST validation result.
- Physical Android execution: pending; no claim of completion was made.

## Privacy

Evidence must not contain account identifiers, private journey content,
credentials, or tokens. The recorder requires a human privacy-review
confirmation but does not claim to automate visual PII inspection.
