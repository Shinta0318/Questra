## Implemented

- `GuildRepository` provides membership lookup, public Guild join/leave, bounded post creation/list/delete, and report operations.
- All mutations use authenticated RPCs; direct table writes remain revoked.
- New Guild posts start as `pending` and are visible only to their author until moderation approves them.
- Approved posts are limited to active Guild members. Private Quest, Trail, and contact data are never copied into a post.
- Owner-only post deletion and bounded report reasons are enforced in the database.
