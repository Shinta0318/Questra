## Finding

The QST-571 public probe required a generic `401 / invalid_credentials` response and exact CORS origin. The older Hosted Beta `auth-login` function also satisfies that contract, so a successful connected-preview startup could be mistaken for verification of the newer login decision and error handling. This does not establish why the account owner's login failed.

## Change

- The local `auth-login` adds a non-secret `x-questra-auth-contract: 2026-10-18.1` response header, including on public entry responses.
- The connected-preview probe compares that marker. An older or missing marker emits an explicit warning while preserving ordinary UI-preview startup. `-RequireCurrentAuthContract` fails closed for candidate verification and cannot be combined with `-SkipRemoteProbe`.
- The public probe still submits only an empty identifier/password; it does not attempt a real login, inspect a password, or grant access.
- The runbook distinguishes warning-only visual preview from a strict deployment check. A matching header would prove only this advertised contract, not database migration parity or a successful login.

## Verification

- `pwsh -NoProfile -File tools/qst/test_connected_preview.ps1`: 13 scenarios passed, including current marker, legacy warning, strict failure, and skipped-probe rejection.
- `dart run tools/qst/verify_connected_preview_launcher.dart`: passed, including local source/probe marker consistency.
- Beta `run_connected_preview.ps1 -ValidateOnly -Port 5268`: Auth/Data/entry probes passed, but printed `source contract UNVERIFIED` and a legacy warning.
- Beta `run_connected_preview.ps1 -ValidateOnly -Port 5268 -RequireCurrentAuthContract`: exited 1 with `Hosted auth-login contract is older than the local candidate`. This is the expected negative result, not a successful Hosted deployment.
- No real account credential, token, or personal identifier was sent by these probes. The connected preview can still render its login page, but cannot be called same-candidate verified.

## Release implication and rollback

External Beta remains NO-GO. After a reviewed, immutable candidate deploys `auth-login`, rerun strict validation and then a user-operated or disposable-account login plus database owner check. Reverting the local marker/probe restores entry-only behavior; it does not change Auth data or existing sessions. Do not remove the warning or bypass strict mode merely to get a green gate.
