# QST-122 Report: Beta First Quest Experience

## Summary

Polished the first Quest creation experience so a new beta user can understand
what to write, what happens after saving, and how Arc helps turn the Quest into
Mission candidates.

## Changes

- Added first Quest guidance to `QuestFormScreen`.
- Added concrete hint/helper copy for Quest title and reason fields.
- Added an empty-title SnackBar so users are not left without feedback.
- Added a short post-save expectation: Arc guide and Mission candidates are
  generated after saving.
- Added widget tests for first Quest guidance and empty-title feedback.

## Acceptance

- A new beta user sees a three-step first Quest flow.
- The Quest title field includes examples and reassures that one sentence is
  enough.
- The screen explains that Arc guide and Mission candidates appear after saving.
- Empty title submission shows a clear Japanese prompt.
- Arc remains framed as a companion/navigator, not an assistant.
