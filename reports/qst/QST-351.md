## Summary

Created the Japan wedge validation package without adding billing or a hard
paywall. The document defines candidate cohorts, plain-language aliases, Arc
tone modes, monthly pricing hypotheses, validation gates, and prohibited
implementation boundaries.

- Candidate cohorts include students, working adults, parents, 40-60 beginners,
  and AI-resistant users.
- First-use aliases pair Questra terms with ordinary Japanese.
- Pricing hypotheses are limited to validation: ¥480, ¥780, and ¥980.
- No real billing or paywall implementation is added.
- Premium boundaries preserve the basic Quest -> Mission -> Task -> Trail
  progression for free users.
- Verifier prevents obvious billing/paywall SDK integration from entering this
  validation QST.
