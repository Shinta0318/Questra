# QST-270 Master Spec Task Domain Alignment Report

## Result

Completed on 2026-08-08.

The product constitution, domain relationship document, and Design Bible now
share one hierarchy:

```text
Quest -> Mission -> Task -> Trail
```

- Quest is the desired outcome or challenge.
- Mission is a verifiable intermediate outcome or route checkpoint.
- Task is the smallest concrete action and belongs to exactly one Mission.
- Trail records evidence, learning, progress, and reflection after action.

## Completion Integrity

Mission progress may be derived from Task state. A Mission is not completed
automatically when required Tasks reach 100%. The user must explicitly confirm
the Mission success condition or expected output. Task completion also never
completes a Quest by cascade.

## Legacy Migration Rule

Legacy Missions that represent concrete actions remain readable. A derived
outcome Mission can group them and the old rows become Task candidates only
after a user-visible review. IDs, completion state, and Trail relationships are
preserved through compatibility mapping; ambiguous conversions require user
approval.
